{{ site.title_long | default: site.title }} builds models that recover clean images and video from degraded captures, and that generate new ones under precise control. Denoising, deblurring, outpainting and super-resolution — from RAW sensor data to broadcast video — that recovers what was really there.

02 Synthesis

Controllable generation and editing, and synthetic data that actually trains detection, segmentation and anomaly models.
